# How to Log In as a Member

Once you have a member account provided by the admin, you can log in to Image Tea to activate your membership access.

## Steps to Log In

1. Open Image Tea.
2. Click the **Member** menu in the menu bar.
3. Click **Login**.
4. A login dialog will appear. Enter your **Email** and **License Key**.
5. Click the **Login** button.

If your credentials are correct, Image Tea will confirm that you are now logged in. Your membership status will be reflected in the application.

## After Logging In

Once logged in, the Member menu will change to show two options:

- **Check Limit** - View your current usage and remaining quota
- **Logout** - Log out of your member account

## Login Errors

If your login fails, check the following:

- Make sure your email and license key are correct (both are case-sensitive)
- Make sure you have an active internet connection
- Contact the admin if you believe your credentials are correct but login still fails

## Staying Logged In

Image Tea remembers your login session as long as the application is running. If you close and reopen the application, you will need to log in again.
