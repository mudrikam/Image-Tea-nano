SotongHD lite

SotongHD lite is a helper tool for uploading images to Picsart AI Image Enhancer and automatically downloading the enhanced results. This extension streamlines the process so you can quickly improve your images and get the results without manual download steps.

  • The extension will automatically upload and download the enhanced image for you.
  • This tool does not store or process your images outside Picsart.
